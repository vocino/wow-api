# Destruction Warlock — SBA-First Mythic+ Build

Goal: Single-Button Assistant abilities stay the highest priority, every pick
makes them stronger, and the build adds as few new rotational buttons as
possible. Mythic+ is the default content. Patch 12.1, Midnight Season 2.

Hero tree: **Diabolist**. Per Icy Veins it is the better M+ choice at any key
level (stacked cleave and AoE via Diabolic Rituals and Diabolic Oculi);
Hellcaller lost too much AoE in 12.1 and is the single-target/spread-cleave
tree. Diabolist also keeps Immolate (Hellcaller's Wither replaces it).

Point budgets assumed: 34 class / 30 spec / 13 hero at level 90 (The War Within
base plus Midnight's +3 class / +4 spec / +3 hero), with 4 spec points reserved
for the Apex section. Picks are ordered top-down so row gates pass naturally.

## Class tree (34)

Core defenses and M+ utility. No rotational buttons here.

- Burning Rush, Soul Leech, Demon Skin 2, Fel Armor 2, Fiendish Stride —
  movement plus the Soul Leech absorb engine.
- Demonic Embrace, Demonic Fortitude, Fortified Soul, Demonic Resilience,
  Soul Link 2, Pact of Gluttony — stamina, absorb caps, redirect, reusable
  Healthstone.
- Horrify (over Nightmare) — feared enemies tremble in place instead of
  pulling more packs.
- Banish, Curse of Exhaustion, Foul Mouth (Exhaustion hits all nearby
  enemies), Blight of Tongues (AoE caster slow) — M+ crowd control suite.
- Mortal Coil + Improved Mortal Coil — horror plus self-heal.
- Demonic Circle + Abyss Walker, Demonic Gateway, Soulburn (macro it to
  Gateway for instant placement; also empowers Circle teleports).
- Dark Pact + Frequent Donor (shorter cooldown), Dark Accord (shorter
  Unending Resolve).
- Pact of the Annihilan (+2% crit), Pact of the Satyr (+2% haste), Pact of
  the Eredar (+3% Intellect).
- Teachings of the Black Harvest (stronger Felhunter dispel and pet bonuses).
- Howl of Terror (over Shadowfury) + Oppressive Darkness — completes the
  Diabolist mass-disorient package (25s cooldown, up to 10 targets with
  Annihilan's Bellow).

Skipped: Curse of Tongues (Blight covers it), Fel Domination, Fel Pact,
Infernal Beneficiary, Gorefiend's Avarice, Empowered Drain Life, Empowered
Healthstone, Pact of the Nathrezim, Swift Artifice, Frequent Traveler, Fel
Synergy, Blight of Weakness (take instead of Tongues on melee-heavy weeks).

## Spec tree (30)

Everything feeds Chaos Bolt, Conflagrate, Incinerate, Immolate, Cataclysm,
and Summon Infernal. Only two new rotational buttons: Rain of Fire and
Shadowburn.

- Chaos Bolt, Conflagrate, Improved Conflagrate (+1 charge), Backdraft
  (Conflagrate speeds up Incinerate/Chaos Bolt), Explosive Potential
  (faster Conflagrate cooldown).
- Rain of Fire, **@target version** (spell 1214467, no ground reticle).
- Roaring Blaze (Conflagrate spreads Immolate — the M+ engine), Scalding
  Flames (+25% Immolate damage and duration), Ashen Remains 2 (+6% to Chaos
  Bolt/Shadowburn/Incinerate on Immolated targets).
- Fire and Brimstone (Incinerate cleaves), Cataclysm, Lake of Fire
  (Cataclysm leaves burning ground that boosts Immolate).
- Shadowburn + Fiendish Cruelty (crits grant free any-target Shadowburns).
- Improved Havoc + **Mayhem** (over Havoc): passive cleave with higher
  uptime and no extra button.
- Summon Infernal + Crashing Chaos (Infernal empowers the next 8 Chaos
  Bolts) + Inferno (30s shorter cooldown, +3% Mastery).
- Improved Chaos Bolt, Chaos Incarnate, Conflagration of Chaos (Conflagrate
  and Shadowburn always crit and scale with crit), Ruin 2 (+30% crit
  damage), Devastation 1 (+3% crit), Diabolic Embers (Incinerate generates
  double fragments).
- **Dimensional Rift** (over Soul Fire): free Chaos/shadow damage procs off
  Chaos Bolt and Shadowburn, no button.
- Reverse Entropy (over Internal Combustion): free Haste procs with no
  interaction cost — Internal Combustion eats Immolate uptime SBA already
  struggles to maintain.
- Summoner's Embrace (over Grimoire of Sacrifice): +3% to everything while
  keeping the Felhunter out for interrupt and dispel.

Skipped: Practiced Chaos, Emberstorm, Flashpoint, Chaotic Inferno,
Backlash, Devastation rank 2, Demonfire Infusion, Alythess's Ire, Avatar of
Destruction, Destructive Rapidity, Raging Demonfire, plus the losing side
of every choice node (Havoc, Soul Fire, Channel Demonfire, Internal
Combustion, Rain of Chaos, Grimoire of Sacrifice). Closest flex swaps:
Backlash 2 in place of Devastation 1 + Explosive Potential for the
instant-Incinerate procs; Havoc over Mayhem for on-demand cleave; Soul
Fire over Dimensional Rift for single-target shard generation.

## Hero tree: Diabolist (13 of 14)

Take everything except Gloom of Nathreza (weakest link: +7% only on
Havoc-marked targets for single-target spells):

- Diabolic Ritual, Touch of Rancora, Secrets of the Coven (Incinerate
  becomes Infernal Bolt), Diabolic Oculi, Looks That Kill, Mind's Eyes.
- Abyssal Dominion (empowered Infernal that splits on expiry), Ruination
  (Pit Lord turns Chaos Bolt into an AoE meteor + imps), Cloven Souls,
  Flames of Xoroth.
- Annihilan's Bellow (Howl package) over Soul-Etched Circles.
- Infernal Machine (shard spending during Infernal accelerates Rituals)
  over Cruelty of Kerxan.
- Infernal Bulwark (Unending Resolve grants a Leech shield) over Infernal
  Vitality; required pathing, and the shield fits M+ burst damage.

## Button map

SBA sequences its known kit: Immolate, Incinerate, Conflagrate, Chaos Bolt,
Cataclysm, Shadowburn, Rain of Fire. You manage the rest:

- Summon Infernal on cooldown, aligned with burst windows and Crashing
  Chaos (SBA can fire it, but timing it yourself is the biggest manual
  gain).
- Rain of Fire and Shadowburn if SBA skips them; both are on your bars.
- Interrupt (Felhunter Spell Lock) and dispel (Devour Magic) — pet is
  Felhunter in dungeons.
- Defenses and utility: Dark Pact, Unending Resolve, Mortal Coil, Howl of
  Terror, Demonic Circle, Gateway (+Soulburn macro), curses, Banish,
  Healthstone. Fear as backup CC.

New rotational buttons versus the SBA core: two (Rain of Fire,
Shadowburn). Everything else new is utility, defense, or movement.
